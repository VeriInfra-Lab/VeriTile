The former public Contract.lean, Correct.lean, and FPEquiv.lean are preserved byte-for-byte as HostContract.lean, HostCorrect.lean, and HostFPEquiv.lean. Their namespaces and historical scope remain unchanged. Proofs/Contract.lean imports the preserved HostContract so it does not depend on the new kernel-only public entry. The kernel revision proves the three JIT bodies at prepared argument tensors; it does not claim wrapper, allocation, aggregation, autograd, or CUDA-launch execution.
